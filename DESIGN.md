## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a balance between academic authority, museum-grade curation, and narrative intrigue for *Archaeology of the Gospels* (*Archeologia Ewangelii*). The aesthetic synthesizes the precision of university press monographs (such as Harvard University Press and Oxford Academic) with the visual storytelling of *National Geographic* and classical biblical archaeology field journals.

The emotional target is reverence, intellectual rigor, tactile curiosity, and literary permanence. The UI avoids sterile academic austerity and commercial sensationalism. Instead, it frames archaeological fieldwork, ancient epigraphy, and material culture through a tactile, archival lens.

Key visual pillars include:
- **Archival Warmth:** Parchment-toned substrate fields that eliminate harsh digital whites, evoking cotton rag paper, unbleached papyrus, and stone rubbings.
- **Editorial Micro-Detailing:** Hairline framing borders, Roman numeral markers, cartographic coordinates, and precise typographic ornaments (fleurons, rules, and brackets).
- **Artifactual Presence:** Layout framing inspired by museum catalog specimen plates, archaeological stratigraphy charts, and illuminated marginalia.

## Colors

The palette derives from physical artifacts: terracotta pottery sherds, oxidized bronze coins, carbon ink scripts, and Levantine limestone.

- **Primary (`#9A5B20` - Burnished Bronze / Terracotta Gold):** Used for focal interactive states, primary calls-to-action, key chapter numerals, and verified research badges. Supports a lighter tint (`#C27D38`) for hover transitions and illuminated accents.
- **Secondary (`#2C2824` - Deep Obsidian Scholar Ink):** The dominant body typography and primary line framing color. Grounded by an absolute deep value (`#1A1815`) for high-contrast display headlines and formal seals.
- **Tertiary (`#5A6552` - Muted Olive / Levantine Slate):** Sourced from ancient olive groves and patinated bronze, this tone serves cartographic markers, reference tags, cross-index anchors, and contextual footnotes.
- **Neutral (`#F4EFE6` - Deep Antique Sandstone / Aged Parchment):** The foundation canvas. Paired with `#FDFBF7` (Virgin Parchment) for elevated content cards, inset plates, and modal dialogues, and `#D4C5B9` (Weathered Limestone) for borders and structural division rules.

## Typography

The typography unites three distinct typographic traditions:
1. **Classical Epigraphy & Display:** **Playfair Display** introduces high-contrast serifs, sharp brackets, and chiseled dignity for titles, section headers, and specimen names.
2. **Bookish Humanist Long-Form:** **EB Garamond** acts as the primary voice for all scholarly prose, synopses, and descriptive text. Its historical provenance delivers natural cadence and effortless readability over long sessions.
3. **Fieldwork & Cartographic Indexing:** **Space Grotesk** is deployed strictly in micro-doses (all-caps labels, catalog indices, coordinates, and metadata badges). This technical juxtaposition introduces the clinical rigor of modern archaeological science alongside classical literature.

Typographic Rules:
- Titles rendered in `label-caps` must use uppercase transformations with tracking between `0.12em` and `0.18em`.
- Scholarly blockquotes in `body-lg` use an authentic italicized variant of EB Garamond, accompanied by a double-ruled vertical border on the left.
- Ensure proper Polish diacritics support (ą, ć, ę, ł, ń, ó, ś, ź, ż) across all weights of Playfair Display and EB Garamond for the bilingual publication edition.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model draws inspiration from classical book design, specifically the *Van de Graaf canon* and symmetrical museum vitrine layouts. The structure relies on a 12-column grid system optimized for scholarly reading, side-by-side artifact comparison, and bilingual synchronicity.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, fixed maximum canvas container of 1360px, outer margins of `4rem`, and gutters of `2.5rem`. Editorial text wells adhere to a constrained 8-column center track (max 72ch) to maintain optical reading comfort, while artifact plates and interactive map viewers break out into full 10- or 12-column widths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, outer margins of `2.5rem`, gutters of `1.5rem`. Margins compress proportionally; bilingual comparisons reflow from side-by-side to synchronized tabbed views.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, outer margins of `1rem`, gutters of `1rem`. Secondary academic apparatus (footnotes, coordinate strings) tuck beneath specimen cards into collapsible drawers or expandable toggles.

Rhythm is derived from an 8pt base grid. Spacing is intentional and open, preventing dense research material from feeling claustrophobic.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system rejects synthetic floating drop-shadows and glassy UI metaphors. Depth is established through **archival stratification**—the physical layering of parchment sheets, intaglio debossing, and museum matting borders.

- **Level 0 (Field Canvas):** `#F4EFE6` (Antique Sandstone) serves as the desk surface.
- **Level 1 (Parchment Folio):** Elevated cards, plates, and chapter containers sit on `#FDFBF7`. Depth is achieved via a hairline inset border (`1px solid rgba(44, 40, 36, 0.12)`) and a subtle warm ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(44, 40, 36, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(44, 40, 36, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Specimen Vitrine & Overlays):** Interactive dialogs, full-resolution artifact lightboxes, and country selector popovers use double-line bounding borders (a 1px hairline outer frame, a 2px spacer, and an inner 0.5px hairline), paired with `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -4px rgba(26, 24, 21, 0.16)`.
- **Intaglio / Debossed (Sunken Inset):** Archaeological excavation notes, stratum identifiers, and metadata chips sit in a sunken surface tier (`#E9E2D5`) with an interior edge: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 2px rgba(44, 40, 36, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometric philosophy is strictly **architectural and bibliophilic**. A roundedness level of `0` (Sharp) anchors all primary structural components. 

Corners reflect cut paper, bindery board, trimmed printing stock, and dressed field ashlar blocks. Curves are permitted only in specific cultural motifs:
- **Buttons, Containers, Inputs, and Cards:** Hard, unfilleted corners (`0px` border-radius). Crisp structural edges respect book-binding geometry.
- **Notched Variants:** Selected archival spec cards employ a historic 45-degree corner notch (`clip-path: polygon(...)` or 4px chamfer) to signify archival record cards.
- **Curved Exceptions:** Circular seals, medallion coin stamps, and radio dots use complete organic symmetry (`50%` radius), providing a high-contrast counterpoint to the razor-sharp rectilinear grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Scholarly Action:** Solid deep obsidian (`#1A1815`) or burnished bronze (`#9A5B20`) background with crisp parchment text (`#FDFBF7`). Features a 1px solid accent keyline offset by 2px on hover. Sharp 0px corners, uppercase `Space Grotesk` tracking (`0.12em`).
- **Secondary / Archival Outline:** Transparent background with a 1px solid border (`#2C2824`). Hover fills with `#E9E2D5` with an organic transition (`200ms ease-out`).
- **Editorial Text Link:** `EB Garamond` italicized link adorned with an antique bronze underline sitting 3px below the baseline with a 1px gap.

### Badges & Scholarly Metrics
- **Scientific Evidence Badges (e.g., "100+ Maps & Plates", "65 Peer-Reviewed Sources"):** A two-tone pill or notched rectangle. Left block: `#2C2824` background with an off-white numeral. Right block: `#E9E2D5` substrate with dark obsidian tracking text. Bounded by a 1px outer rule.
- **Strata & Dating Chips:** Monospaced coordinate badges displaying excavation coordinates (e.g., `LOCUS 42 • STRATUM III`) in `label-caps` set against a muted olive background tint (`rgba(90, 101, 82, 0.12)`) with a `#5A6552` border.

### Bilingual Switcher (EN / PL)
- A segmented switch framed in a 1px `#D4C5B9` boundary. The inactive language is styled in muted slate-ink (`rgba(44, 40, 36, 0.6)`), while the active language is highlighted with a parchment background (`#FDFBF7`), sharp rectangular outline, and an authentic micro-seal dot in burnished bronze. Text reads: `EN | PL` or `ENGLISH | POLSKI`.

### Amazon 14-Country Marketplace Selector
- Designed as an archival international trade register.
- **Closed State:** A structured dropdown component showing the selected national market (e.g., `AMAZON.COM (US)` or `AMAZON.PL (POLSKA)`) with an embossed flag seal and a downward-pointing chevron.
- **Dropdown Vitrine:** A structured grid overlay categorized into continental regions (North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific). Each country tile features the regional domain, currency notation, and direct publication format badge (Hardcover / Paperback / Kindle) in crisp hairline framing.

### Artifact & Map Plate Cards
- Built to resemble museum display cases. Images are bordered by an off-white passe-partout matting (`12px` to `24px` border in `#FDFBF7`), bounded by a 1px hairline border (`#D4C5B9`).
- Captions are structured in three tiers: Roman numeral plate header (`label-caps`), historical description (`body-sm`), and scientific citation/inventory number (`caption-citation`).

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Crisp `#FDFBF7` fields with 1px structural borders in `#D4C5B9`. On focus, the border transitions to burnished bronze (`#9A5B20`) with no fuzzy glow; instead, an inner hairline border is applied.
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp square boxes (`18px x 18px`) in `#FDFBF7` with a 1px dark border. Checked state displays a solid diamond or custom bronze cross-stroke rather than a generic digital checkmark.
